Account & sign-in

Sign-in goes through Logto, an OAuth/OIDC identity provider.

The app never sees or stores your password. It stores only the session token Logto issues — on-device, encrypted, in the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore.

What is sent to the server, and why

Wardrobe items, outfit recommendations, favourites, and conversation turns with the Stylist are transmitted to the OnurAI API and stored there, associated with your account, so the app can show your own wardrobe and recommendations back to you across sessions.

Photos

This app accesses your device's photo library — never the camera directly. The one photo you choose to add a garment is uploaded to the backend to support wardrobe and recommendation features. There is no background or bulk photo access.

AI processing

To generate recommendations and replies, your inputs are processed by a language model. Depending on backend configuration, that runs either on infrastructure the operator controls directly, or through a third-party AI provider — in which case content is processed by that third party under its own terms.

AI disclosure

Recommendation and conversational reply text is generated by a language model — no person reviews or writes it. Wardrobe photo recognition and the final outfit-selection logic are, where documented as such, model-independent; the model phrases the reply, while the underlying evaluation is separate.

Data deletion & your rights

The app has a data-transparency screen listing what the Stylist has learned about you, with the ability to delete individual facts. Wardrobe items, favourites, and saved outfits are each independently deletable through their own screens.

Full account deletion is available from the profile screen when signed in, behind a confirmation step. It removes your wardrobe, favourites, saved outfits, imported products (photos included), the Stylist's memory of you, and conversation history.

This does not delete the sign-in identity itself (a separate system, Logto) — removing that account is a separate step with the identity provider.
